

## The Address Book by Mask

House numbers were not invented to help you navigate the city or receive your mail, though they perform these two functions admirably. Instead they were designed to make you easier to tax, imprison, and police. House numbers exist not to help you find your way, but rather to help the government find you.

The premodern state, was in many crucial respects, partially blind; it knew precious little about it subjects, their wealth, their landholdings and yields, their location, their very identity.  But to master a society, they first had to discover who was in it. The state had to create citizen with identities. It had to create citizen with names that could be recorded, with matching address, put down in cadastral survey.

Do you know in England in 1700s 90% of men had on of only eight names: John, Edward, William, Henry, Charles, James, Richard, Robert. It wasn't very useful for tax collector or an outside policeman? only local could know about these people.

And then ruler begam to demand permanent last names,  and then housing numbers 

Address is a way to describe identities to people they record. The use of the address, the practice of attaching identity to residence is , a condition of modernity. And the state had to understand its society, to identify its subjects, before it could do anything to shape it.

House numbers were a natural fit for an Enlightenment Age.  Assigning each house a number simultaneously advanced principles of the enlightenment: rationality and equality. Cities should be easy to navigate, people easy to find. Taxes could be collected, criminals found quickly and a peasant home was numbered the same way as an aristocrats. 

The goal of addresses was to ensure the state see all of it's people.

### Digital Address and Street address

Street address have become one way to remember, and remembering is one thing digital addresses cannot do.

Digital addresses bypass arguments over the meaning of street names. but those argument are what constitutes them as communities. Which is the reason digital address doesn't make communities. In a way they can divide them, your neighbor's what3words address is completely unrelated to yours. You can't learn her address from looking at her house, you have to ask an app, a third party. You can't ask anyone on the street for directions.

Graham Rhind said digital address like what3words don't provide any link between our mental maps and addressing, and removing that link stops addressing being effective. There is no relationship between horse.town.faster and what I experience when I traverse through my world. 

Digital address create a world in which we all exist as dots on a map, each our own island, named by a corporation. Digital address is merely functional and can't replace street address which is imbedded in our relationship and experience
